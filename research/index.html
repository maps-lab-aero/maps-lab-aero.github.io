Research

Research interests

APS Lab develops computational, experimental, and learning-enabled methods for cleaner, quieter, more efficient, and reliable air transportation.

Aerospace propulsion systems

We study gas turbine aerodynamics, heat transfer, turbomachinery performance, and electric or hybrid-electric propulsion architectures for next-generation aircraft.

  • Gas turbine aerodynamics and heat transfer
  • Powertrain sizing and component modeling
  • Mission and performance analysis for DEP, UAV, and VTOL systems

Multidisciplinary optimization

We build design optimization methods that connect high-fidelity physics, reduced-order models, and uncertainty-aware decision making.

  • Projection-based reduced-order modeling for accelerated shape optimization
  • Multi-fidelity techniques for robust aerospace design
  • Uncertainty quantification for aerospace design and optimization

Intelligent control

We develop intelligent control and energy management methods for electrified propulsion, fuel-cell systems, and low-carbon transport platforms.

  • Model predictive control and reinforcement learning for hybrid-electric propulsion
  • Energy management analysis and control for aircraft propulsion systems
  • Vehicle-level energy intelligence for low-carbon transport systems
