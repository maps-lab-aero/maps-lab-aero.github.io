About MAPS Lab

Research at the intersection of computation, propulsion, and system design.

Vision

To shape the future of aerospace propulsion through intelligent multidisciplinary design, enabling cleaner, quieter, more efficient, and reliable air transportation.

MAPS Lab develops computational methods, optimization workflows, and learning-based tools for next-generation aircraft and propulsion systems.

Our work connects turbomachinery, fuel cells, thermal management, controls, and energy systems with modern data-driven design methods.

Research areas

What we work on

Aerospace propulsion systems

Integrated design of propulsion architectures, turbomachinery, and energy subsystems.

Multidisciplinary optimization

Surrogate-assisted optimization, active subspaces, and high-fidelity design workflows.

Intelligent control

Learning-based energy management and adaptive control for fuel-cell and aircraft systems.
